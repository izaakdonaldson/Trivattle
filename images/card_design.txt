Implement a reusable **Trivattle card component** following a clean, futuristic, flat Material Design-inspired aesthetic, matching the card mockups.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. **Header:** Small type icon on the left, followed by the Wikipedia article title. No text label for the type or rarity badge. The rarity should be recognizable from the card's appearance.

2. **Image:** Large rounded rectangular Wikipedia image. If unavailable, use the existing placeholder background corresponding to the card's type. The placeholder background is determined by the card's **type**, not its rarity.

3. **Stats:** Heart icon with HP, shield icon with DEF, displayed horizontally.

4. **Attacks:** Compact rows separated by thin dividers, without individual buttons or backgrounds. Each row contains:
   - **Left:** Small colored icon (20px) representing the attack's type, using the existing type icons.
   - **Middle:** Attack name in bold red text. No description.
   - **Right:** Small icon representing the attack's special effect, followed by the damage number in bold red. **The right icon represents the EFFECT, not the attack type.** If the attack has no effect, omit this icon.

5. **Passive:** If present, display the passive name in blue at the bottom, with a unique blue Lucide icon representing that specific passive. No description on the card itself.

## Effect icons

I haven't created these assets yet. Use simple, consistent Lucide icons for now:

- Burn: `Flame`
- Heal: `HeartPlus`
- Pierce: `Crosshair`
- Weaken: `ArrowDown`
- Splash: `Waves`

## Passive icons

Each passive must have its **own unique icon**. Do not use the same sparkle icon for every passive.

Use these Lucide icons:

| Passive | Lucide icon | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Stalwart | `Shield` | Takes 5% less direct attack damage |
| Recovery | `HeartPulse` | Restores 5 HP once when health drops below 40% |
| Immune | `ShieldCheck` | Removes defensive type weaknesses, making super-effective attacks neutral |

Use blue for passive names and icons, regardless of the card's rarity.

Passive descriptions should only appear in the expanded card view, not on the compact card.

## Rarity styling

**Each rarity should have a distinct visual identity through its background, border, glow, and reflective effects.**

Use the existing card mockups as visual references.

The overall card layout must remain identical between rarities. Only the visual treatment changes.

### Common — White/Gray

- Clean white or very light gray background.
- Subtle gray border.
- Very light shadow.
- No glow, shine, holographic effect, or particles.
- Minimal and flat.
- Should feel like the standard baseline card.

### Uncommon — Green

- Mostly white background with a very subtle green tint.
- Thin green border.
- Very faint green glow around the edges.
- Slightly more vibrant than Common.
- No holographic shine or animated particles.

### Rare — Blue (Shiny)

- Pale blue-tinted background.
- Thin, vibrant blue border.
- Soft blue outer glow.
- **Subtle holographic/foil effect:** A translucent diagonal reflection occasionally moves across the card.
- The shine should be understated and elegant, not distracting.
- Slight reflective highlights near the edges.
- Should feel noticeably more collectible than Uncommon.

### Epic — Purple (Holographic)

- Very light lavender background.
- Vibrant purple border with a soft purple glow.
- **More pronounced holographic effect than Rare.**
- Subtle iridescent reflections, including occasional pink, blue, and purple highlights.
- A slow, smooth animated shine that moves across the card surface.
- Slightly luminous border.
- Keep the text legible and the overall appearance modern.

### Legendary — Gold (Shiny + Sparkles)

- Warm white or very pale gold background.
- Bright gold border with a soft golden glow.
- **Premium holographic finish**, including subtle rainbow reflections.
- Animated reflective shine moving across the card.
- **Small animated sparkling particles** occasionally appearing around the edges and corners.
- Sparkles should twinkle intermittently, not constantly overwhelm the design.
- Slightly stronger glow than Epic.
- Should immediately feel special while maintaining the same clean, flat visual style.

### Rarity animation guidelines

Implement the shiny and holographic effects using lightweight CSS animations where practical.

- Common: No animation.
- Uncommon: Static soft glow.
- Rare: Gentle, occasional reflective shine.
- Epic: Slow holographic shimmer and soft glowing border.
- Legendary: Holographic shimmer, warm glowing border, and intermittent sparkles.

Animations should be smooth, subtle, and performant, especially when many cards are displayed simultaneously.

Prefer animating opacity and transforms rather than expensive visual filters.

Pause or simplify decorative animations for offscreen cards, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Important:** Do not add rarity badges or text labels to the card. Rarity is communicated visually through its color and effects.

## Styling

- Minimal, flat, modern 2026 UI.
- Soft rounded corners, subtle shadows, generous but efficient spacing.
- Dark navy text on light backgrounds.
- Avoid old-fashioned trading card frames or ornate decorative borders.
- Use a consistent layout across all rarities.
- Keep the card compact enough to display multiple cards together.
- Avoid excessive gradients, oversized icons, or unnecessary decorations.
- Attacks remain red regardless of rarity.
- Passives remain blue regardless of rarity.
- Card type icons retain their own established colors.
- Clicking a card opens a detailed view showing attack descriptions, effects, passive information, rarity, and other metadata.

## Important implementation details

Use the actual existing card data, including each card's attacks, effects, passive, and rarity.

Do not hardcode three attacks or assume every card has a passive. The existing rarity rules determine what appears:

- **Common:** 1 attack, no effects, no passive.
- **Uncommon:** 2 attacks, no effects, no passive.
- **Rare:** 2 attacks, either one effect and one passive, or two effects and no passive.
- **Epic:** 2 attacks, both with effects, and one passive.
- **Legendary:** 3 attacks, two with effects, and one passive.

Inspect the repository to locate the existing Wikipedia image placeholders and type icons rather than recreating them.

Make the component responsive and reusable across collections, team selection, and battles.

**The goal is a consistent, sleek trading card design where rarity progressively adds premium visual treatments without making the UI cluttered.**
